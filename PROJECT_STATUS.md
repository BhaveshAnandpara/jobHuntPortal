## Summary

| Area | Done | Partial | Not Started |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Count of items above | 26 | 3 | 5 |

**The big three open gaps**, in priority order:
1. 🟡 **No real job board integration** — automatic discovery only searches a static fixture list.
2. 🟡 **No real outreach delivery** — approved messages are recorded, not actually sent (no SMTP/LinkedIn provider wired in).
3. 🔴 **No re-matching on new resume upload** — existing opportunities aren't automatically re-scored when you add a new profile, contradicting the "previously discovered opportunities may also be re-evaluated" goal in `about_project.md`.

Everything else in the core pipeline — resume parsing, matching, contact
discovery/ranking, outreach generation, human approval, tracking, and the
full frontend — is genuinely implemented and tested, not stubbed.
